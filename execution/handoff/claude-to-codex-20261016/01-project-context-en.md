# OTONOM Project Context

## Product and release intent

OTONOM is an Egypt-first Arabic/English smart-home company site. Its primary commercial objective is engineering-led B2B project contracts; retail commerce is secondary. Approved hardware families are described as 4-, 6-, and 16-channel relay controllers, but commercial SKUs, prices, claims, and warranty terms remain owner-controlled inputs.

## Confirmed scope

- **M2.1 B2B Preview:** marketing, solutions, projects/case studies, consultation and RFQ lead generation. No public checkout and no active customer warranty portal.
- **M2.2 + W1:** later commercial store plus a genuine warranty/service portal, only after warranty, privacy, consumer, shipping, payment, operational, and security gates are approved.
- Personas include developers, contractors, project customers, staff owners/admins, engineers, editors/reviewers, store staff, and later customers.
- Distinct Store and Projects workspaces may share backend/auth/database; “two workspaces” does not mean “two databases.”
- Written client consent is required before identifiable project imagery is published.
- RFQ drawings remain disabled until the private-storage, malware-scan, privacy, and security gates are demonstrably satisfied.

## Implemented locally, subject to fresh verification

- Bilingual public B2B pages and RFQ intake.
- RFQ qualification, organization matching, opportunity, proposal and contract evidence workflows.
- Mutually exclusive Website-Sourced / Website-Assisted attribution and configurable KPI reporting.
- Payload publishing workflow, claims/evidence, consent/media controls, revisions and audit events.
- Staff password + TOTP MFA, role-based API checks, and staff workspaces.
- Commerce/order/inventory/serial and customer/warranty/service/RMA foundations behind PREVIEW gates.

## Proposed or blocked, not approved facts

- Option A architecture remains provisional.
- Primary North Star KPI is unselected.
- FX rounding per contract/adjustment, four-eyes controls, content-return notes, and some workflow controls are implemented proposals awaiting confirmation.
- Prices, warranty duration/exclusions, returns, shipping, payment, privacy/legal text, final brand/content, hosting, e-mail, analytics consent, and production operations remain blocked.
- 3D/2D house exploration, device control, marketplace, configurator, and unified accounts remain deferred.
